The pool was heated but the air was not, and the distance between the two was the whole problem. Claire stood at the edge in her suit and counted.

---

"Just jump," said Marco from the water. His voice echoed off the concrete walls of the hotel courtyard. It was after midnight and they were not supposed to be here.

"I'm going to jump."

"You've been saying that."

"I'm building up to it."

Marco floated on his back and looked at the sky. There were no stars — there were never stars in the city — but the clouds had a soft orange glow from the streetlights below. It was almost beautiful if you didn't think about why.

---

Claire jumped. The water was warm and the shock was not the temperature but the sound — the sudden underwater silence after a night full of traffic and voices and the low hum of the hotel's ventilation system.

She surfaced and pushed her hair back. Marco was grinning.

"See?" he said.

"See what?"

"It's always better than standing at the edge."
