The coffee shop on Seventh had exactly one good table. It was near the window but not against it, close enough to watch the street but far enough that you didn't feel watched. Daniel always sat there.

---

"You're here early," said the barista, whose name was either Amy or Annie. Daniel had never asked and it was now too late to ask.

"Couldn't sleep."

"Bad dreams?"

"No dreams at all. That's worse, I think."

She set the cup down without a saucer, the way she always did. Daniel wrapped both hands around it and looked at the street. A woman was trying to parallel park a car that was clearly too large for the space. He watched her try three times before giving up.

---

His phone buzzed. He turned it face down without looking. There was a particular pleasure in ignoring a phone — the same pleasure, he imagined, that people once got from not answering their doors.

The woman with the large car had found another spot, half a block down. She was walking back now, smiling at something he couldn't see.
