There is a growing body of evidence that boredom is not merely the absence of stimulation but a cognitive state with real utility. When we are bored, the default mode network activates — the same network responsible for autobiographical memory, future planning, and creative ideation.

---

We have engineered boredom out of modern life with remarkable efficiency. Every elevator ride, every waiting room, every moment between moments has been filled with a screen. The question is whether we have also engineered out the mental states that only boredom can produce.

Consider the shower. It remains one of the last boredom-adjacent environments in daily life, and it is no coincidence that people routinely report having their best ideas there. The shower is boring. You cannot check your phone. Your hands are occupied. Your mind wanders.

---

I don't think the answer is a digital detox or some ascetic rejection of technology. The answer is more mundane: just occasionally do nothing. Stare out the window. Walk without headphones. Let the mind wander without giving it a destination.

The returns are asymmetric. Five minutes of genuine boredom can surface insights that five hours of focused work cannot.
